THE THREE PRESSED-IN LOOKS — the part that did not exist

Three different things all want to be shown by pushing a button down, and in the sheet all three looked the same. Each one now has its own job. Tab across this row to see focus, and click the last two to switch them.

  • Clicked travels down, lip gone. Nothing else changes.
  • Keyboard focus never moves. A gold ring outside the black line.
  • Switched on down for good, lit from inside, and says "On".

Focus never moving is what makes it work: a switch you have only tabbed to cannot be mistaken for one you have turned on. And because it never moves, all three can be true at once and still read.

WITH MOTION TURNED OFF

Windows has a setting for people who find movement unpleasant. With it on, these buttons keep every bit of their depth and simply stop travelling: pressing one takes its lip away where it stands. The row below is forced into that mode so you can see it without changing your settings.
