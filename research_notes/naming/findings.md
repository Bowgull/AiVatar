# Naming: Aivatar, Aang, and the browser (2026-10-14)

Not legal advice. US records were checked through mirrors (Trademarkia, Justia); the USPTO site rate-limited the search. The Canadian (CIPO) database needs JavaScript and was not searched directly; EUIPO and WIPO were not reached. Re-check all three before any release.

## 1. Copyright vs trademark
- Names, titles and short phrases cannot be copyrighted in the US ([Circular 33](https://www.copyright.gov/circs/circ33.pdf)). Canada treats titles very narrowly too ([Osgoode](https://yorku.ca/osgoode/iposgoode/?p=7183)).
- For a NAME the risk is trademark. A well-defined CHARACTER (Aang) is protected by copyright ([DC v. Towle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DC_Comics_v._Mark_Towle)).

## 2. "Aivatar"
- AIPARK (South Korea) holds a REGISTERED US mark AIVATAR, serial 98764027, for AI software ([Trademarkia](https://www.trademarkia.com/aivatar-98764027)). Their AiVATAR avatar-video product is a [CES 2026 honoree](https://www.ces.tech/ces-innovation-awards/2026/aivatar/).
- Gerald Isaac Music Group has PENDING AIVATAR applications covering software and online services ([search](https://www.trademarkia.com/search/trademarks?q=aivatar)).
- "Avatar" alone is a dictionary word for a user's picture ([Merriam-Webster](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/avatar)); about 559 US software marks contain it. The link to Paramount comes from pairing it with Aang and the airy look.
- Verdict: fine for personal use; low to moderate risk shared for free; rename before selling (and replace Aang and the Avatar look then too).

## 3. Aang, Appa, Momo
- Paramount (via Viacom) filed AANG and APPA; both ABANDONED ([AANG](https://www.trademarkia.com/search/trademarks?q=aang)). The franchise names are registered.
- The bigger risk is copyright on the character. Free fan projects still get takedowns ([Fan game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fan_game), [Techdirt](https://www.techdirt.com/2021/01/15/nintendo-hates-you-gaming-giant-lobs-dmca-nuke-hundreds-fan-games)).

## 4. Browser names (zero exact US marks found, no existing app found)
Best fit and lowest risk first: Driftleaf, Windwick, Gustling, Breezling, Kitewood, Hearthwind, Windloft.
Weaker: Breezewood, Thistledown, Whirligig.
Rejected (taken or too close): Dandelion, Pinwheel, Aerie, Featherloft, Skyhollow, Zelda words like Skyloft.

## 5. Bottom line
- Aivatar: keep for personal use; rename before selling.
- Browser: top three are Driftleaf, Windwick, Gustling.
