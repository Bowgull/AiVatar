# Browser logo differentiation (2026-10-14)

Design research, not legal advice. His verdict on the plum-and-autumn badge: "it still reminds me of Firefox".

## Why it read as Firefox
- Mozilla found people recognise Firefox "without a fox" ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Firefox_logo)). The brand lives in "warm orange shape wrapped on a purple round field" (purple since 2019, [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2019/06/11/mozilla-gives-firefox-a-new-logo-as-it-looks-beyond-the-browser)).
- At 16 to 32 px a leaf and a fox tail blur into the same orange curl. Colours and shapes are separate brand cues; orange + purple is the one pair Firefox owns ([Azrights on Romaniuk](https://azrights.com/blog/protecting-brand-identifiers-what-we-can-learn-from-jenni-romaniuk), [WARC](https://www.warc.com/content/article/building-and-employing-distinctive-brand-assets/121642)).
- Circles are the browser convention: 12 of 14 browsers were round in 2006 and "very difficult to tell apart" ([Macworld](https://www.macworld.com/article/181578/circular.html)).

## What is taken
Orange on purple or a ring: Firefox. Purple alone: Tor (#7D4698). Four colours in a circle: Chrome. Blue-green wave: Edge. Orange-red lion on a shield: Brave. Red O / red V: Opera / Vivaldi. Red bug: Ladybird. Orange circle: DuckDuckGo. Blue circle/compass: Safari, LibreWolf.

## Small-icon rules
- Silhouette first, few shapes, distinctive outline ([Microsoft](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/apps/design/style/iconography/app-icon-design)). One idea ([Apple HIG](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/app-icons)).
- Dark, middle and light values; light from the top-left; 3:1 contrast on light and dark.
- Let the object's own outline be the icon. Test: 16 px, greyscale, squint.

## Colour options (his wood, gold, parchment + one accent)
1. Jade #2F7D6D / #1E5247: nobody owns it.
2. Oxblood #7A1F2B: medium risk (reds are taken, but bright).
3. Sky blue #7EC8E3 on wood: low to medium.
4. Ink #2B2118 on parchment #EADBB8 + gold: lowest risk, most hand-made.
5. Moss #5E7A3A + gold: low.
Keep plum for inside the app, not the logo.

## Shapes (no circles)
Paper kite (best at 16 px), wooden signpost (strong), paper lantern (good), maple seed glider (medium), scroll (medium), feather (poor).

## Name
No software called Driftleaf found. "Leaf" pulls toward common round leaf marks; the name can stay if the leaf is not the whole logo. Alternatives (web search only): Driftkite (clear), Lampwick (clear; a Pinocchio character), Kitewood (Kiteworks nearby), Wayfern (Wafrn nearby), Hearthlight (some risk). Avoid Paperkite, Waymark, Lantern.

## Top 3
1. Driftkite: parchment diamond kite on wooden sticks, jade leaf on the sail, gold ribbon tail.
2. Lampwick: cream paper lantern, dark wood cap, gold glow, ink + parchment.
3. Driftleaf: wooden signpost with a moss-green leaf-shaped arrow board edged in gold.
