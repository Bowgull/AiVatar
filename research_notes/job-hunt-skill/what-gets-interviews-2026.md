# What gets interviews in 2026 (deep research, 2026-10-14)

Objective review of his job scan against current evidence. Vendor data is marked; weak evidence is said so.

## Climate
- Canada flat: 510,200 vacancies Q2 2026, rate 2.8% ([StatCan](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/daily-quotidien/260625/dq260625a-eng.htm)); postings -2.8% y/y ([Indeed Hiring Lab](https://www.hiringlab.org/en-ca/2026/07/28/q2-2026-canadian-labour-market-update-steadying-out/)); "low hire, low fire" ([HCAMag](https://www.hcamag.com/ca/news/general/canadian-labour-market-outlook-for-2026-stability-not-strength-report/560794)). Toronto unemployment 6.9% June 2026 ([StatCan](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/daily-quotidien/260710/t008a-eng.htm)).
- CS specifically: 44% of companies had CSM layoffs, 42% froze hiring (secondhand, weak) ([CustomerThink](https://customerthink.com/?p=1080033)).
- Applications per job +102% 2022-2024 ([Greenhouse](https://www.greenhouse.com/newsroom/greenhouse-launches-dream-job-to-fix-job-hunting-and-boost-candidates-chances-of-getting-hired)); fewer than 7% of applicants interviewed (vendor) ([Greenhouse](https://www.greenhouse.com/uk/newsroom/greenhouse-has-entered-into-a-definitive-agreement-to-acquire-ezra-ai-labs)).
- Ghost jobs: 18-22% of postings lead to no hire ([QZ on Greenhouse](https://qz.com/one-in-five-job-postings-is-fake-ghost-jobs-1851738201)).
- Ontario since 2026-01-01 (25+ staff): say if a posting is an existing vacancy, pay range no wider than $50K, disclose AI screening ([Littler](https://www.littler.com/news-analysis/asap/canada-new-ontario-job-posting-requirements-force-january-1-2026)).
- Screening is mostly humans plus knockout questions, not robots auto-rejecting (small vendor study) ([BetaNews](https://betanews.com/2025/11/10/study-debunks-viral-claim-that-ai-filters-out-most-job-applicants/)).

## Channels
- CareerPlug: job boards 61% of applicants / 42% of hires; career pages 13% / 26%; referrals 2% / 11% ([CareerPlug](https://www.careerplug.com/recruiting-metrics-and-kpis/)).
- Ashby: inbound still ~half of hires; referrals ~17-18% and convert better ([Ashby](https://www.ashbyhq.com/talent-trends-report/reports/inbound)). Referred applicants more likely to get offers ([NY Fed](https://www.newyorkfed.org/medialibrary/media/research/staff_reports/sr568.html)).
- 60 cold applications with zero interviews: bad, but at a 2-5% base rate about 1 run in 6 gets zero.
- Early: ~50% of hires applied in week one, 27% within two days ([ERE/StartWire](https://ere.net/post-a-job-this-week-your-hire-has-probably-already-applied)). His 72-hour window fits.
- Company career sites vs LinkedIn: ~11% vs ~3% interview rate (vendor, weak) ([Huntr via BU](https://questromfeld.bu.edu/blog/2026/03/10/announcing-the-9-best-job-search-sites-job-boards-of-2026-backed-by-600k-applications/)).

## Applications
- Tailored resumes ~1.6x (self-reported) ([Huntr](https://huntr.co/research/2025-annual-job-search-trends-report)); editing help raised hires 8% in an RCT ([NBER](https://www.nber.org/papers/w30886)); AI-tailored letters lose signal value when everyone uses them ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.25054v1)); generic AI resumes rejected by many managers ([TopResume](https://topresume.com/career-advice/ai-in-hiring-survey)).
- AI auto-apply tools: no independent outcome data; reports of invented skills ([Resumly](https://resumly.ai/best/best-ai-job-search-tools)).

## Coverage
- Aggregators heavily overlap (Indeed dedupes across the web; SimplyHired and Glassdoor share Indeed's parent). Low value from boards 5-14.
- Company ATS boards (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby) are public, first, and need a target-company list ([jobspipe](https://jobspipe.dev/sources/greenhouse.md)).

## Verdict: top 8 changes by expected impact on interviews
1. Referral / warm-intro finding per shortlisted role (connections and alumni at the company), asks drafted for his approval, no automated messaging.
2. A 60-120 company target list with public ATS feed monitoring and same-day lane alerts.
3. Fewer, stronger applications (3-5 a week), honestly tailored resume plus a specific "why you" line.
4. A short hiring-manager/recruiter note after applying, drafted for approval (weak evidence, cheap).
5. Ghost-job and fit filter: skip postings older than ~21 days or repeatedly reposted; flag Ontario "not an existing vacancy"; note missing pay ranges.
6. Dedupe across boards and cut to LinkedIn, Indeed, Job Bank.
7. Community and recruiter track (CS Slack communities, a few Toronto agencies).
8. Interview prep pack as soon as a reply arrives.
Track reply rate by channel, not application count; if ATS-direct plus referral does not beat 5% after ~30 applications, revisit positioning.
