# Aang's page in the browser: research (2026-10-14)

His call: the desk window moves into the browser, opened on demand, no permanent tab or trail. He asked for research on entry points, where it sits relative to Settings, and one tab vs many.

## Ways in
- Shortcuts are an extra way in, never the only one ([NN/g, UI accelerators](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ui-accelerators/)). Search works with visible navigation, not instead of it ([NN/g, Search Is Not Enough](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/search-not-enough/)).
- Duplicate links cost attention; copies are OK only when different users look in different places ([NN/g, duplicate links](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/duplicate-links/)).
- Browsers: menu item + shortcut + typed address per internal page (Chrome Ctrl+H/J, Firefox about:addons, Arc's command bar, Vivaldi panels). ChatGPT memory: Settings > Personalization > Memory, or just ask ([OpenAI](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8983136-memory-faq)).
- Recommendation: 1 visible key in the top bar, ask Aang, links from context (bubble, alerts), a shortcut. Avoid copies (sidebar + key, menu item copying the key, tray entry, permanent tab).

## Next to Settings?
- Chrome keeps History/Downloads (things you use) apart from Settings; Firefox keeps add-ons apart from preferences. Microsoft: settings are for rarely changed options ([Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/apps/design/app-settings/guidelines-for-app-settings)).
- Pick: Aang's page separate, with his own settings section last, and one link row each way between it and the browser's Settings.

## One tab with sections vs tabs
- "The fewer tabs, the better"; no stacked rows ([NN/g, Tabs Used Right](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/tabs-used-right/)). A side list scales and scans faster ([NN/g, vertical nav](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/vertical-nav/)). Top rows cap at about 5 or 6.
- Aang has 8 sections and growing: one tab with a side list, each section with its own address for deep links.
- Games: Ocarina/Majora 4 subscreens switched with Z/R; Stardew one menu with ~10 tabs, opens on Inventory, Options near the end ([Zelda Wiki](https://zeldawiki.wiki/wiki/Subscreen), [Stardew wiki](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Player_Menu)).
