# Showcase scripts: Aang, Samara, Waymark

2026-10-22. For Joshua to read aloud. Tools and workflow: `docs/SHOWCASE-TOOLS.md`. Shots and press kit: `docs/SHOWCASE-PLAN.md`.

**How to read these:**
- About 150 words a minute. Times are a guide; your real takes set the final timing, and the video follows your voice.
- **Say** is what you read. **Screen** is what's on screen while you say it.
- Cross out any line that doesn't sound like you. Swap words freely. Just don't add features that aren't built (the list of what's safe is at the bottom).
- Read each block as one take. Pause a beat between blocks; the cuts go there.
- Everything shown runs on demo data. No real names, mail, sites or training log.

## Fact check: what the scripts rely on (all built, as of 2026-10-22)

**Aang**
- **Typing box.** Ctrl+Plus opens it (clicking Aang does too).
- **Speech bubble.**
  - Comic bubble that grows when it needs room.
  - First words in about half a second.
  - Click shows the rest.
- **Claude Code marks.**
  - The mark over his head works like a WoW quest mark: needs-you is pale blue, job complete is a teal tick.
  - Clicking the mark shows the jobs, and he can answer Claude's questions there.
- **Wooden chimes.** One plays at JOB COMPLETE.
- **Pop-outs.** The hourglass timer has real sand physics. Notes and the Spotify mini player are also built. The window family snaps together.
- **Morning brief.**
- **Quiet next to games.** Messages are held, not dropped.
- **"Can't see himself" line.** It is in the README.
- **The README's own lines.** "ai tools wrote the code, i was the very picky boss" and "still building".
- **Animations used.** idle, hello, talk, think, zip, spin, nap, look.

**Samara**
- **Plan card.** It shows the plan before it starts; you can untick a site or tell Claude what to change.
- **Claude's tabs.** It has its own grouped tabs and a big STOP key.
- **Claude rune.** It shows job progress.
- **Shield and site rules.** Bank and money sites are never opened; they open in Chrome with one button.
- **Page tricks blocked.** Hidden trick text can't reach Claude, and pages can't trick it into pressing Send.
- **Pop-outs.** Videos pop out, the tab shows what's playing, and closing the pop-out brings Samara back.
- **Mockups trail.** Every version is kept.
- **Version names.** Every big version is named after a character; the current one is 1.21.0 "Gojo".

**Waymark**
- **Morning Report.** The drum picker logs it.
- **Six-week blocks.** Block Zero is week 4 of 6 in the demo, and missed sessions roll forward.
- **Bag Work.**
  - Rounds come prebuilt, with a reason for each round.
  - There's no START ROUND button any more (2026-10-22). The clock waits, and tapping the grass starts it.
  - Labels: FIGHT in gold, FINISH in the last 10 s, REST in teal.
  - Sounds: synthesized boxing bells and the 10-second clap.
- **Strava.** Runs become "run evidence".
- **Coach.** It is "bounded", adapts inside rails, and explains changes.
- **Ledger.** Rings, streak, PRs (demo: a 14-day streak).
