# Samara QA, 2026-11-19

A full engineering and QA pass over Samara (src/Shell and src/Samara), on local `main` at 04fee871, with the
fullscreen and standalone-tools merges included. It answers Joshua's four questions with evidence.

How it was run:
- Everything visible ran on a hidden Windows desktop (`tools/hidden-desktop-run.ps1`). A window there can never draw
  on his screens, go fullscreen on them or take their focus.
- Every run used throwaway profiles and state.
- His real profile was only read, through `\\localhost\C$`.
- Nothing ran on the Mac. His live Aang was not touched or restarted, and nothing was merged, pushed or released.

The PC's screens, found first:
- `DISPLAY1`: the primary, 1920x1080 at 0,0, monitor `BLD1000` "ES-G24F4". This is his main screen.
- `DISPLAY2`: 1920x1080 at -1920,0, monitor `BLD06AB` "SB220Q". This is the Acer.

No check was put on the Acer. Where a check needed a real screen and the Acer was the only option, it was skipped,
because Samara can force a window to the front (`bringFront`, browser.ts:337 via winedge.ts:300), and that could take
focus from his main screen.

- `samara-qa-privacy-code.md`: every outbound destination with file:line evidence.

## The verdicts

### 1. Is Samara a standalone Chrome/Firefox rival?

**Standalone: yes, proven.** Samara runs with Aang off, with no window, through its own `Samara.exe`. It survives being
killed and is restarted by its launcher (samara-alone-check 7/7, samara-exe-check 6/6, samara-down/quit/apart checks
all green).

**A rival to Chrome or Firefox: not yet, for four reasons.**
- **Paid video was broken on both machines until this branch** (F1).
- **No DevTools by design**, and print, find-in-page, zoom and Chromium's picture-in-picture are untested.
- **Passkeys are a no-go** (PASSKEYS-GO-NO-GO, 2026-11-18). Banks are sent to Chrome.
- **It uses about 550 MB of private memory for one page** (Chrome uses far less), and it is one engine version behind
  because castLabs signing is stuck (B-7).

It is a capable everyday browser with its own features: pop-outs, the secret window, ad blocking and Aang. But it still
hands his hardest sites to Chrome.

### 2. Does it keep his data private?

**Better than Chrome, behind Firefox and Brave in four measured ways.**

What it does well:
- No telemetry, crash reports or analytics. Nothing goes from Samara itself to Anthropic or Discord. This was checked
  in the code and on the wire: a fresh Samara left alone for three minutes sent none of this.
- While browsing, only the visited site's own hosts were contacted.
- It blocks ads and trackers with uBlock's lists and sends Global Privacy Control. The privacy check passed 10/11.

Where it falls behind (P-1 to P-4 in the fix list):
- **Login cookies are stored unencrypted.** The castLabs engine runs with `EnableCookieEncryption` off. This was read
  from `engines/castlabs-44.5.1/electron.exe` today, and it applies to `%APPDATA%\Electron\Network\Cookies`.
- **The secret window keeps its data.** It is a PIN-locked profile kept on disk, not a private window.
- **The secret window writes into shared settings files.**
- **Background pings reveal his IP and that he is online:** ntfy is always connected, GitHub is checked every 15
  minutes, and Reddit every 30 minutes with his cookies.

### 3. Do ALL its functions work?

**No.**

Of 48 Electron checks:
- 36 pass completely, counting four that only log what happens (startup, video full screen, paid video, idle cost)
  and were read by hand.
- 5 have failures that are real or not yet explained: pop-out size, privacy hints, mockups, video resume spot, and one
  Claude-driving step.
- 6 failed only because the hidden desktop can't give a window focus, a real click or a taskbar (5), or because the
  test is stale (hotkeys).
- 1 could not run: the tab-engine check needs a prepared folder.

Bugs found:
- **Paid video:** dead since 2026-11-12. Fixed here (F1).
- **Pop-out:** does not shrink its video when the window shrinks (B-2).
- **Esc in full screen:** probably swallowed while Aang's bubble shows (B-1).
- **Secret window on the PC:** has no way back from a forgotten PIN (B-3).
- **Mockups page:** 11 failures (B-6).
- **Autofill, pop-under blocking and login catching:** could not be shown working on the hidden desktop. They need a
  focused window or a real click, so they remain unproven, not proven broken.

### 4. Has every pop-out interaction been looked at and tested?

**Looked at: yes, all 30 windows and pop-outs** (inventory rows 1-30).

**Tested on the PC:**
- Mini player, notes, timers, notices, tear-out, theater, pop-out return, drag and any-player: all green.
- Pop-out size: 10 of 11.
- Taskbar: 25 of 31. The 6 failures need a real taskbar.
- Video full screen and its Esc: work.
- Secret window: 25/25. Secret full screen: 11/11.
- Passkey/Chrome offer: 14/14.
- Paid-video window: opens since F1.

**Not tested:**
- Chromium's own picture-in-picture, print, site pop-ups' placement (they open unmanaged on the primary display, B-8),
  two-monitor placement, and minimise.
- The castLabs sign-in itself. It needs his password, and it is failing (B-7).
- Everything on the Mac (see the Mac section).

## Bugs and risks found, not fixed (ranked fix list)

"Live" means seen running. "Code" means found by reading and not reproduced.

1. **P-1: Cookies unencrypted at rest (Live: the fuse was read).**
   - Repro: read the fuses of `engines/castlabs-44.5.1/electron.exe`: `EnableCookieEncryption 0`, `RunAsNode 1`,
     `EnableNodeOptionsEnvironmentVariable 1`, `EnableNodeCliInspectArguments 1`.
   - engineupdate.ts:312-317 puts castLabs' own switches back, and 257-261 refuses an engine with encryption on.
     tools/fuses.mjs:28 wants encryption.
   - Any program running as him can read every login cookie, and `RunAsNode` lets any program use Samara's exe as a
     Node interpreter.
   - Fix: flip the fuses before castLabs signing (the signature covers the exe), then re-sign. That needs a working
     castLabs sign-in (B-7), so it is not small.
2. **B-1: Esc probably can't leave a full-screen video while Aang's bubble shows (Code).**
   - main.ts:313 registers Esc system-wide whenever the bubble shows. Since Samara runs apart, that global hotkey
     takes Esc before Samara's window sees it, and the press closes the bubble instead.
   - Repro on the Acer, with his OK: put a YouTube video full screen in Samara, have Aang speak, press Esc.
   - Fix: let go of the global Esc while any Samara window is full screen, or have Samara own Esc then.
3. **P-3: The secret window writes to shared files (Code).**
   - Files: `site-rules.json` (browser.ts:1692), `adblock-paused.json` (1700/1764), `download-trusted.json` (1545),
     `browser-http-ok.json` (6306), `device-ok.json` (1259).
   - Normal Samara loads them and syncs them to the Mac (browser.ts:4753).
   - Cookie let-throughs go to the shared `cookie-pass.json` and to `privacy-log.jsonl`, which Claude reads
     (privacy.ts:94-111).
   - A secret tab's crash or hang writes its host to `samara.log` (flight.ts:59).
   - Repro: in the secret window, pause ads on a site, then open normal Samara's shield on that site: it shows as paused.
   - Fix: guard each write with `if (this.o.secret)` and keep secret-only copies. Several small changes; it needs a
     decision on whether secret rules should carry over at all.
4. **B-3: The secret PIN can never be reset on the PC (Code, certain).**
   - `macPassword()` (browser.ts:502) returns false off the Mac, so after a forgotten PIN or five wrong ones (2366,
     2452) the PC's secret window stays locked for good.
   - This breaks the two-machines rule.
   - Fix: on Windows, ask Windows Hello / the account password through `UserConsentVerifier` or `CredUIPromptForWindowsCredentials`.
5. **B-7: Engine updates are stuck, and they retry every 15 minutes forever (Live, his log).**
   - Since 2026-11-18 01:09, `samara.log` has a line every 15 minutes:
     `castLabs signing said signin (exit 1) ... NonInteractiveError: 'Password' attribute has no default value`.
   - 44.11.4 is downloaded, but its own test failed 4 of 29 (the UPDATE key's position, hover text, amber colour and
     panel). 44.9.3 sits unpromoted.
   - Fix: castLabs sign-in, which needs his password in a real terminal; then back off after repeated sign-in failures
     instead of every 15 minutes.
6. **B-2: The pop-out video does not shrink with its window (Live, popout-size-check).**
   - The window went 868 → 1268 → 768 px wide, and the video went 884 → 1284 → stayed 1284.
   - Untriaged. `follow()` (popout.ts:105) only follows a lent tab. The view also reads 16 px wider than the window,
     which suggests a DPI or frame offset.
7. **B-6: Mockups page, 11 failures (Live, mockups-check).**
   - NEW marks are not taken off when a sheet is opened, the gem count is wrong (3, not 1), and the NEW count is wrong.
   - Sheet 119's tab-scrolling edges (left/right edge, mouse wheel, End and Left keys) failed.
   - The test was last changed 2026-11-09. Untriaged: either the page or the test drifted since.
8. **B-8: Site pop-ups are unmanaged (Code).**
   - browser.ts:2944 lets Electron make the window with no position or parent, so it opens on the primary display
     with focus and is not closed with its tab.
   - It is the one Samara window that can land on his main screen uninvited.
9. **P-4: Background pings tie his IP to "online now" (Live, network capture).**
   - ntfy.sh is always connected, GitHub every 15 minutes, Reddit every 30 minutes with his cookies, ad lists every
     4 hours, and Google's Widevine updater.
   - None has an off switch, and pausing ads does not stop the list downloads.
10. **P-2: The secret window is not a private window (by design, sheet 87).**
    - Its kept profile `persist:aang-secret` and its plain-JSON history and tabs in `%APPDATA%\Aang\secret` stay on
      disk.
    - Before the PIN, typing still goes to Yandex suggestions through the persistent default session
      (browser.ts:5293).
    - Worth telling him plainly: it locks, but it does not forget.
11. **P-5: Sync carries every login cookie as JSON over `ws://` (Code).** The connection is macmain.ts:253 and
    browser.ts:1133. It is only as private as Tailscale, which does encrypt it in transit.
12. **B-9: Netflix gets browser hints without a platform (Live, privacy-check step 8).** Low.
13. **B-10: A broken paid-video start hangs instead of failing cleanly (Live).** Without `stack.ts` the castLabs
    process threw on load and then stayed alive until it was killed. On a real desktop that is probably an Electron
    error box on his screen, but this was not shown.
14. **B-12: Every ad list was fetched twice at start (Live, network capture).** For example, `filters.txt` twice
    within 0.1 s. ads.ts:178 is meant to share one download, and the cause is not traced. About 6 MB extra per start.
15. **Housekeeping:**
    - A stray `browser-sync.json.9528.233.tmp` (48 KB, 2026-11-01) sits in `%APPDATA%\Aang`.
    - The Chromium profile is Electron's default `%APPDATA%\Electron`, because samara.ts never names it, so any
      unnamed Electron program would share it.
    - No crash reporter, so a crash can't be told from a kill: 6 unclean exits on 2026-11-17/18, mostly updates and
      restarts that night.

Test-only fixes still owed:
- `hotkeys-check` should expect the Satchel on Ctrl+Alt+V.
- `notes60-check`, `tray-check` and `tabengine-check` take their output folder from the last argument, so run without
  one they try to write a folder named after the test file itself.
- Several checks (`popunder`, `autofill`, `login-catch`) pass vacuously or fail when a click or focus never lands. They
  should assert that the click arrived.
