## The verdicts

### 1. Is Samara a standalone Chrome/Firefox rival?

**Standalone: yes, proven.** Samara runs with Aang off, with no window, through its own `Samara.exe`. It survives being
killed and is restarted by its launcher (samara-alone-check 7/7, samara-exe-check 6/6, samara-down/quit/apart checks
all green).

**A rival to Chrome or Firefox: not yet, for four reasons.**
- **Paid video was broken on both machines until this branch** (F1).
- **No DevTools by design**, and print, find-in-page, zoom and Chromium's picture-in-picture are untested.
- **Passkeys are a no-go** (PASSKEYS-GO-NO-GO, 2026-11-18). Banks are sent to Chrome.
- **It uses about 550 MB of private memory for one page** (Chrome uses far less), and it is one engine version behind
  because castLabs signing is stuck (B-7).

It is a capable everyday browser with its own features: pop-outs, the secret window, ad blocking and Aang. But it still
hands his hardest sites to Chrome.

### 2. Does it keep his data private?

**Better than Chrome, behind Firefox and Brave in four measured ways.**

What it does well:
- No telemetry, crash reports or analytics. Nothing goes from Samara itself to Anthropic or Discord. This was checked
  in the code and on the wire: a fresh Samara left alone for three minutes sent none of this.
- While browsing, only the visited site's own hosts were contacted.
- It blocks ads and trackers with uBlock's lists and sends Global Privacy Control. The privacy check passed 10/11.

Where it falls behind is kept in his private aang-data repo (security/samara/samara-qa-2026-11-19-privacy.md), not in
this public repo.

## Bugs and risks found, not fixed (ranked fix list)

"Live" means seen running. "Code" means found by reading and not reproduced.

The privacy items (P-1 to P-5) are kept in his private notes, not in this public repo.

1. **B-1: Esc probably can't leave a full-screen video while Aang's bubble shows (Code).**
   - main.ts:313 registers Esc system-wide whenever the bubble shows. Since Samara runs apart, that global hotkey
     takes Esc before Samara's window sees it, and the press closes the bubble instead.
   - Repro on the Acer, with his OK: put a YouTube video full screen in Samara, have Aang speak, press Esc.
   - Fix: let go of the global Esc while any Samara window is full screen, or have Samara own Esc then.
2. **B-3: The secret PIN can never be reset on the PC (Code, certain).**
   - `macPassword()` (browser.ts:502) returns false off the Mac, so after a forgotten PIN or five wrong ones (2366,
     2452) the PC's secret window stays locked for good.
   - This breaks the two-machines rule.
   - Fix: on Windows, ask Windows Hello / the account password through `UserConsentVerifier` or `CredUIPromptForWindowsCredentials`.
3. **B-7: Engine updates are stuck, and they retry every 15 minutes forever (Live, his log).**
   - Since 2026-11-18 01:09, `samara.log` has a line every 15 minutes:
     `castLabs signing said signin (exit 1) ... NonInteractiveError: 'Password' attribute has no default value`.
   - 44.11.4 is downloaded, but its own test failed 4 of 29 (the UPDATE key's position, hover text, amber colour and
     panel). 44.9.3 sits unpromoted.
   - Fix: castLabs sign-in, which needs his password in a real terminal; then back off after repeated sign-in failures
     instead of every 15 minutes.
4. **B-2: The pop-out video does not shrink with its window (Live, popout-size-check).**
   - The window went 868 → 1268 → 768 px wide, and the video went 884 → 1284 → stayed 1284.
   - Untriaged. `follow()` (popout.ts:105) only follows a lent tab. The view also reads 16 px wider than the window,
     which suggests a DPI or frame offset.
5. **B-6: Mockups page, 11 failures (Live, mockups-check).**
   - NEW marks are not taken off when a sheet is opened, the gem count is wrong (3, not 1), and the NEW count is wrong.
   - Sheet 119's tab-scrolling edges (left/right edge, mouse wheel, End and Left keys) failed.
   - The test was last changed 2026-11-09. Untriaged: either the page or the test drifted since.
6. **B-8: Site pop-ups are unmanaged (Code).**
   - browser.ts:2944 lets Electron make the window with no position or parent, so it opens on the primary display
     with focus and is not closed with its tab.
   - It is the one Samara window that can land on his main screen uninvited.
7. **B-9: Netflix gets browser hints without a platform (Live, privacy-check step 8).** Low.
8. **B-10: A broken paid-video start hangs instead of failing cleanly (Live).** Without `stack.ts` the castLabs
    process threw on load and then stayed alive until it was killed. On a real desktop that is probably an Electron
    error box on his screen, but this was not shown.
9. **B-12: Every ad list was fetched twice at start (Live, network capture).** For example, `filters.txt` twice
    within 0.1 s. ads.ts:178 is meant to share one download, and the cause is not traced. About 6 MB extra per start.
10. **Housekeeping:**
    - A stray `browser-sync.json.9528.233.tmp` (48 KB, 2026-11-01) sits in `%APPDATA%\Aang`.
    - The Chromium profile is Electron's default `%APPDATA%\Electron`, because samara.ts never names it, so any
      unnamed Electron program would share it.
    - No crash reporter, so a crash can't be told from a kill: 6 unclean exits on 2026-11-17/18, mostly updates and
      restarts that night.

Test-only fixes still owed:
- ~~`hotkeys-check` should expect the Satchel on Ctrl+Alt+V.~~ The Satchel was removed on 2026-11-20, so Ctrl+Alt+V is
  free again and the test stands as written.
- `notes60-check`, `tray-check` and `tabengine-check` take their output folder from the last argument, so run without
  one they try to write a folder named after the test file itself.
- Several checks (`popunder`, `autofill`, `login-catch`) pass vacuously or fail when a click or focus never lands. They
  should assert that the click arrived.
