Sheet 92 · Samara's notices

Today every notice is the same plain box under the toolbar, whether it's good news, a problem or something that needs you. Here are three new looks, each in Samara's own wood, parchment and pixel art. Each one has three kinds: done, needs you, went wrong. Tap a kind to see it play.

FEEL A · GAME TEXT BOX

A wooden dialogue box, like Stardew and Zelda. The letters type in, and a little arrow bobs when it's done.

The icon's plate carries the colour of the kind. It fades after 6 seconds; a "needs you" stays until you act.

FEEL B · ITEM GET

A dark plaque drops out of the band, like "You got the Hookshot!". The icon lifts up with a sparkle, then it slides back.

The biggest moment of the three. Best for things worth celebrating, a bit loud for small stuff.

github.com

FEEL C · LANTERN TAG

A paper tag swings down on a cord from the band, lit by Samara's lantern. The lantern glows in the kind's colour and flickers.

The quietest of the three. Ties notices to Samara's lantern icon.
