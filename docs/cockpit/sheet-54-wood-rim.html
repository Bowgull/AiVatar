How these were made
  • The bubble is the app's own page. The rim is drawn from the bubble's real outline, so it bends round the corners and the tail on any size.
  • The wood is Samara's own painted wood, pixel for pixel, at exactly 2×. The corners, rivets, knots and curls are hand-placed pixels.
  • A rim is a little bigger than today's thin line, so the bubble sits 8 to 12 pixels further from Aang. The tail keeps its length.
