How these were made
  • The notes are new, so they are drawn: every paper, ribbon, tape, stud, tick box and switch pixel placed by hand at exactly 2×.
  • Aang's bubble (and its keys), his right-click menu, and Samara's frame and ... menu are the app's real pages and styles. The page inside Samara and the game behind the quick note are made up. The rail is new, drawn with Samara's own wood.
