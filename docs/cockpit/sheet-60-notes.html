How these were made
  • The notes are new, so they are drawn: every paper, rim, pin, tick box and switch pixel placed by hand at exactly 2×, with Samara's own wood pictures.
  • Aang's bubble lines, his right-click menu and Samara's frame are the app's real pages and styles. The page inside Samara and the game behind the quick note are made up.
