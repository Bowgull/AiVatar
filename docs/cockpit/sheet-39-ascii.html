Sheet 39: ASCII in Aang

Every picture here is drawn live from light and depth, then turned into text characters, the way that ASCII MMORPG does it. All of them move. Switch the style at the top and every scene redraws in it. Aang himself stays pixel art. Scenes pause when off screen, and in the app they would pause while a game is in front.

A dusk meadow with a lantern and fireflies.
