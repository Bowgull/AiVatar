SHEET 47 · THE MOCKUPS TRAIL

Every mockup Claude opens goes to Samara, into one MOCKUPS trail with Claude's icon on it. A newer version of a sheet updates its own tab (the small V3 says which version you are looking at, and opens the others). At the bottom of the trail, HISTORY lists every mockup and every version, newest first, closed ones too. The trail's broom removes exact duplicates only, and only when you press it. Older versions open exactly as they looked, because each one is kept the moment it is opened.
