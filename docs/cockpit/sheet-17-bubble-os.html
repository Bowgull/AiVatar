What's going on here

The wheelThe first mockup's fan of wood discs, with your six: Timer, Notes, Browser, Music, Snip, Quiet. Hold the mouse on Aang, let go on one.
TimerSay it ("set a timer for 25 minutes for the pizza") or set it on the kitchen dial: drag the knob, or roll your mouse wheel on it.
The hourglassDrawn pixel by pixel: wood caps, two posts, glass, and sand that falls through the neck. Drag it anywhere. +1, pause, stop on it. Right-click for see-through and pin.
NotesFree text. "Hey make a note for raid: flasks, food" turns commas into ticks. Click the box at the start of a tick line to tick it.
Right-click a noteColour (the pin matches), bold, italic, underline, strike, header, ticks, bullet and numbered lists, size, font (plain, handwritten, fancy, pixel), and on the screen: see-through, pin, put back.
On the screenPull it out of the bubble for real. Drag it by its top, resize from the corner. They all start the same size.
PinLike the pop-out: your mouse goes straight through it to the game. Hover it and press Ctrl+Alt+P to unpin. Same key as the pop-out: it pins or unpins whatever is under your mouse.
