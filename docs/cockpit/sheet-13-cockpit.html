What's going on here

One shape, any sizeAlways his comic balloon. It grows or shrinks, and the tail keeps pointing at him.
Everything lands in the balloonSnips, highlights, files, folders and links all arrive here. He asks what you want. Nothing goes anywhere on its own.
Four places it can goAsk Aang about it, give it to a running Claude session, black out private bits, or have him remember it.
Read, never obeyedAnything you hand him is text to read. If a page says "ignore your rules", he treats it as words on a page.
Plain words, not codeApprovals say what Claude wants in plain English. The exact command is one tap away.
Safe buttonsNew buttons ignore clicks for a moment. Things that can't be undone need a hold, not a click.
Chat details keptReading pace, file chips, right-click a reply, scroll up for older chats, copy and rate on hover.
Qwen reads firstFiles, folders and links are read by Qwen on this PC. Claude only when you say go deeper.
