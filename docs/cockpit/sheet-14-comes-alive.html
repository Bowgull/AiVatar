What new art would add

Code gets most of the way. PixelLab art would add the moves code can't fake. Roughly 165 of a month's 2,000 tries.

In-between movesSit, then nap. Nap, then a startled wake. Hand to chin to think. So he eases between moves instead of snapping.
Getting ready and settlingA crouch before a hop, a settle after landing, a lean-in before he talks.
Head turnsA three-quarter head turn, mirrored for the other side, so he can really look at the bubble or at you.
Small idle movesA yawn and stretch, scratching his head, a tiny air swirl in his palm, a foot tap, a look over his shoulder.
ReactionsA flinch when poked, dangling when picked up, a happy bounce, a surprised jump.

How it works: each frame is drawn on his real 112-pixel grid, then doubled, exactly like the app. Blinks and eye moves recolour a few of his own eye pixels. Timing ideas from coucou's Mochi (random blink every 2.2 to 5.4 s, 22% double blinks, happy after 1.9 s of hovering, dizzy after 3 clicks in 1.7 s), clawd-on-desk's idle ladder and VPet's weighted idle choice.
