2 · THE FINGER, LESS CHUBBY

Today's hand is a 12 × 9 drawing with a big fist and a one-pixel finger, and the bubble stretches it to 26 × 20, which is not a whole number (2.17×): some pixels come out twice as wide as others. Half the chubbiness is the art, half is that stretch. Every redraw below is drawn at exactly 2×, with the dark-wood outline everything else uses.

3 · A FUZZIER, WARMER AURA

Today the aura is his silhouette stamped into a hard 3-pixel ring whose brightness rides a sine: the ring never changes shape, so it reads as a solid colour fading on and off. All three takes below are soft at the edge and breathe in size and brightness together, 4 seconds, eased, never fully off. Live, on his real idle frame, shown at 2× (whole pixels) so you can see the edge; the colours are the marks' own.

B · Warm bloom is my pick
  • B keeps a thin white-hot line hugging him, so he still pops off a busy game, and the colour turns into soft haze around it: warm, like a lit lantern.
  • A is the softest: pure haze, no edge. Lovely on a plain desktop; can go muddy over a bright WoW zone.
  • C adds slow little sparks rising off him. Charming, but moving bits in the corner of your eye over a game are the most distracting.
  • How the pet draws it, cheaply: the same trick as today, his silhouette stamped as rings, but 10 to 15 rings instead of 1, each fainter the further out. The rings are worked out once per frame size and cached; each tick only changes how bright they are drawn, so it costs about what today's ring costs. Nothing touches his own pixels.

4 · ONE TYPE SYSTEM FOR EVERY BUBBLE

Nine jobs, nine looks, the same everywhere Aang speaks. Ratios are measured against the darkest corner of the parchment and the lightest wood (WCAG AA: 4.5 : 1 for normal text, 3 : 1 for large). Only bundled fonts: Figtree for reading, Silkscreen for small labels (at 8 px, its own pixel grid, so it stays sharp), IM Fell English for a few styled titles, Consolas for Claude's exact words.
