SHEET 41 · THE SCROLL-BACK KEEPS EVERYTHING (v2)

Built from your brief (sheet 24) and the plan card (sheet 37): the Lantern's wood, parchment slips for each part of the day, a pixel icon on every row, your three key colours, and the turquoise glow for anything you said yes to. Scroll up on any bubble, even a one-line one, and it turns into this. The bubble below is the real one, tail and all. Try it: scroll up on the bubble, click a row, hover for Reply, click Aang. Any link he shows you, like a meeting link, is underlined with Samara's lantern in front: click it and it opens in Samara.

3 · WHAT YOU SAID YES TO

Every yes keeps a turquoise YOU SAID YES tag and says what actually happened. Open it for Undo (burnt orange); it asks once, then the row stays in the history with a gold UNDONE tag, so you can always see what happened and when. An "always" gets Take it back. Something that can't be undone, like a sent email, is grey CAN'T UNDO and says why instead of offering a button.

4 · REPLY TO ANYTHING

Hover any row: a gold REPLY key takes the place of the time. The row lights up and your typing box opens with it pinned in the chip it already uses. Aang gets the whole thing (the video, the brief, the plan), not just its words, and answers in the bubble.
