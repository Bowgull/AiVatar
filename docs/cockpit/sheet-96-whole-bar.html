Sheet 96 · The whole bar, as one thing

You're right that it feels disconnected, and the lantern alone won't fix it. I measured today's bar from its own files. Every piece was added on a different sheet, in its own style. So it reads as parts stuck on wood, not one thing.

FOUR MATERIALSPixel wood, smooth purple buttons, a flat black address bar, and gold icons with no button at all.
THREE PIXEL SIZESThe wood is stretched to 2.2x tall, the icons are 2x, the lantern was 1x. Pixels that don't match look pasted on.
COLOURS FIGHTPurple, navy, gold, cream and red all at once. Game menus use a few colours from one family.
NO HOME FOR THINGSKeys float on the wood. Claude, downloads and updates pop in wherever there's room.

A · ONE CARVED PLANKMY PICK

The whole bar is one plank of Samara's wood. Every key is carved into it, with brass icons. The address bar is a groove cut along it.

The lantern hangs in its own niche at the left, as Samara's crest, and opens the menu. Claude's rune is set into the end of the groove. One wood, one brass, one cream, every pixel the same size. Purple goes away.

B · INVENTORY BAR

Like Stardew's toolbar or Zelda's item buttons: a row of the same square slots, each holding one tool. The address bar is a parchment strip in the middle.

The most "game" of the three. Every slot is the same, so new things (downloads, an update, Claude) just take the next slot. Busier than A.

C · QUIET LINE (I'D PUSH BACK ON YOU HERE)

Maybe the bar is trying too hard. A thin dark rail, a gold edge, brass icons with no buttons that light up when you point at them.

Like a modern game's HUD: it stays out of the way, so the page and your mockups get more room. The lantern is small at the left. Least wood, least charm.
