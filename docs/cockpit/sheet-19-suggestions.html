What changes in all three

No gloveThe chosen row is a highlight bar with a thin gold edge, the way every list in a browser marks it. The glove stays where it means "your cursor is here" in the drawer.
A glyph, not a labelA tiny pixel glyph on the left says what each row is: magnifier for a search, a tab, the star for a bookmark, a clock for history, an arrow back for a closed tab. Same 7 by 7 grid as the band's keys.
What you haven't typed is boldChrome's rule. Your eye jumps to the part that's new, so you see what Enter will finish.
One word, only on the chosen row"Switch to tab", "Search", "Open" shows at the right of the chosen row only, so the list stays calm.
Lined up with the fieldThe list is exactly as wide as the address field and starts at its left edge, so it reads as the field's own list.
