SHEET 45G · CLICK AANG

Exactly your words, drawn: click Aang and his chat box opens with the folded progress bar of every running job right above it. Click a bar to unfold its tasks. The purple key on a bar points your chat box at that session: send now, or queue it for after the task it is on. Replies, plan changes and questions come back through Aang's bubble, in plain words, with the keys to act. Nothing new over his head: he stays in his calm idle while Claude works; only his marks show when something needs you.

1 · THE WHOLE THING (PRESS PLAY)

2 · STEP BY STEP

CLICK AANG: THE BOX, AND YOUR JOBS ABOVE IT

Each job is its folded bar: Claude's icon, the session, what it is on, the percent, the time left. Typing straight away just talks to Aang, like now.

CLICK A BAR: ITS TASKS UNFOLD

The tracker you liked, inside the bar: finished tasks ticked, the current one with its percent, the rest with how long they usually take.

THE PURPLE KEY: TALK TO THAT SESSION

The box gets a terracotta TO CLAUDE · Waymark chip, so you always know where your words go (the × goes back to Aang). Send now or After this task.

QUEUED, THEN ANSWERED

A queued message waits on its bar in lavender. When Claude answers, the bubble shows what changed (added, moved) with Looks good / Change it; Claude's own words one click away.

CLAUDE WANTS TO CHANGE ITS PLAN

The bubble becomes the change: what it wants to add and why, with Add it / Skip / Tell it why.

A QUESTION, WITH THE BOX CLOSED

You are elsewhere: only his lavender question mark shows, just over his head. Click him and the bubble shows the question with its keys.

3 · THE SAME FOR EVERYTHING AANG DOES

The folded bars over the chat box are not only Claude's. Anything Aang is busy with sits there the same way, and the purple key always means "talk to this one":
  • The job hunt run: its bar, and the key lets you say "skip anything in Toronto".
  • Research Aang is doing: "go deeper on the second one" while it runs.
  • Files Aang is making, the Waymark refresh, a Mac update: bar, time left, the key to tell it something.
  • Email drafts and plans waiting for you: the bubble shows them with their keys, the same way it shows Claude's questions.
