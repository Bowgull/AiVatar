SHEET 118 · KARAOKE FLOW

v1 · 1 Nov · three ways for the lyrics to flow; pick one

Today a line just jumps to the next. Here the words move with the song. The words below are made up; their timing has a real song's shape, with quick lines, slow ones and a gap.

A · The lines glide

All the lines sit in one column that glides up as the song goes. The line being sung grows and lights up; the ones before and after fade out at the edges.

B · The words fill with gold

One line at a time, with gold filling it from left to right as it's sung. The next line waits underneath and rises in to take its place.

C · Both

The gliding column, with the line being sung filling with gold.
