How these were made
  • The player is new, so it is drawn: every wood, paper, key and icon pixel placed by hand at exactly 2×, using Samara's own wood pictures and the bubble's paper colours.
  • The seek and volume bars are the app's own round progress bar (gold, cream). The pin is the job pop-out's pin. Round corners are drawn pixel by pixel, never smoothed.
  • The four scenes (sheet-59/scenes.js) are made-up pixel paintings, 34 × 34 (the cover's size), built the way the pixel-loop artists build theirs (Waneella, Kirokaze, Mark Ferrari), not copied: a small palette per scene, ONE light source and everything else in shadow, figures lit on the side facing the light, light as flat bands (no noisy dither), and small layered motions (flame, sparks, fireflies, mist; the push and four street layers; rain, drops, tail, steam; the cast, glitter, waves, palm). This page draws them live at 10 fps; they would ship as small sprite sheets, the street layers as endless strips.
  • The campfire is Waymark's world (its page-14 clearing and page-33 road): pale trunks in green-black fog, the rune arch with vines at the end of the red-dirt path, moss, mist, fireflies and the cyan motes, in Waymark's own fog and moss colours. Looked at, not copied: nothing was taken from the Waymark repo.
  • In section 4 the video pop-out (with 12 px corners, the one change asked for) and the Claude job are drawn by the app's own styles. The cover art is a made-up pixel painting.
