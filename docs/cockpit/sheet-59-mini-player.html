?WHAT EACH PART DOES

PinThe same pin as the job pop-out. Pin one pop-out and they all stay on top. Hover by it for the see-through slider; every pop-out follows. ShadeDouble-click anywhere (or the small bar key) for the thin strip: scrolling title, play/pause, a progress line. Double-click again to come back. VisualizerThe VIS / VU key. Off by default if you prefer it calm; it remembers. Seek and volumeDrag the gold bar to jump; the volume is Spotify's own. Where the song comes fromSpotify's official sign-in, plus Windows' "now playing" so it shows the song even before you sign in.
How these were made
  • The player is new, so it is drawn: every wood, paper, key and icon pixel placed by hand at exactly 2×, using Samara's own wood pictures and the bubble's paper colours.
  • The seek bar is the app's own progress bar in gold. The pin is the job pop-out's pin.
  • In section 4 the video pop-out and the Claude job are drawn by the app's own styles. The cover art is a made-up pixel painting.
