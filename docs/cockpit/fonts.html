THE PATTERN — THIS IS THE FINDING

1. Every single one pairs a characterful DISPLAY face with a plain READABLE body face. Not one of them uses a single font for everything.

2. Zelda and Mario both reach for classical inscription capitals at the top. Charlemagne and Lithos are both carved-in-stone faces. That is not coincidence — it is the genre saying this is old and this matters.

3. The body text is deliberately boring. A clean sans, or a pixel font built for legibility at one exact size. The character lives in the display face and the frame — never in the paragraph.

Aang today does the opposite of all three. One font for everything — Atkinson Hyperlegible, an accessibility face engineered to have no character — plus a pixel font misapplied to buttons at 8px. Decoration where legibility is needed, and no display face at all. That is why it reads flat.
