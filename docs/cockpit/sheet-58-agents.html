SHEET 58 · BACKGROUND HELPERS

When Claude sends out helpers (its background agents), you see them under their job, never as a job of their own. Three simple ways to show them. The example: your Waymark job with three helpers running.

WHAT TO PICK A (my pick), B or C.

Your questions, answered

Does my progress bar include them? Partly. When Claude stops and waits for a helper, the job's bar already waits for it too (its time left won't run out before the helper's). Helpers that run in the background while Claude keeps going don't move the bar. That's right: they're side work, so they get their own little line under the job instead.

Job or helper? A helper. It belongs to its job, sits under it, and goes away when it's done. The job's own bar stays exactly as it is today.

What kinds are there? Research is the most common. Aang sorts every helper into one of six, from what Claude called it:

Research: looking things up on the web or in docs
Explore: searching and mapping the code
Build: making or changing code
Check: tests, reviews, making sure it works
Design: mockups and pictures, like this sheet
Plan: working out the approach first

How far along? If the helper keeps its own to-do list, you see its % (Build 62%). If not, how long it has run, and how long that kind usually takes here (Research 4 min of about 6).

How these were made
  • Every picture is the real thing drawn by the app itself: Aang's speech bubble with the jobs from Claude's mark, Samara's rune and its panel, and the popped-out job. Only the helper row is laid on.
  • The icons are 10 by 10 pixel-art pixels, each 2 screen pixels, with the same dark-wood outline as Claude's mark.
  • A bar drawn paler is a guess from time (that kind of helper usually takes about this long); a solid one is a real % from the helper's own to-do list.
