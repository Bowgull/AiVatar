SHEET 46 · ONE COCKPIT

One bubble, one engine. Everything lives inside it, bottom to top: the typing area (its bottom edge), the jobs strip, the one thing waiting on you (pinned, with "1 more"), and the conversation, which is also the scroll-back: scroll up and you are in yesterday. When Aang speaks up by himself it is just his one line; click it, click him or press your key and it opens around that line. It grows and shrinks to what is inside, wider for a video, never past the screen, never a scroll bar (the bobbing gold arrow says there is more).

4 · ONE ENGINE, EVERY MESSAGE

Every message is built from the same parts and the engine lays them out: who (Aang, or Claude with the session's tag), words, choices (the glove), keys, details (open on click), Claude's words. Adding a new kind of thing later (email, job cards, research) means adding a card, not a new window.
  • Zones, bottom to top: typing area, jobs strip, pinned (most urgent first, "N more"), conversation. Only the conversation gives up room; it never drops below a few lines.
  • Sizes: one line when unasked; opened, as tall as it needs up to about two thirds of the screen (lower with WoW in front); width follows the content.
  • Motion: the springy half-second for opening, about a third of a second for small changes, cards fade up as they arrive.
  • Looks are skins: A, B and C are the same engine and the same cards with different paint, so you can switch, or we make more.
