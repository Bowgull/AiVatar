SHEET 52 · A CLAUDE JOB ON YOUR DESKTOP

Pull a job out of Aang's bubble and it floats on your desktop as just the job, with no window around it. This sheet shows how it looks, how it comes and goes, its states, and your new question: how you make it see-through (section 6).

1WHAT IT LOOKS LIKE

The bar from Aang's bubble, cut loose. No title bar, no frame, no close button. Grab it anywhere to move it.

2CLICK IT AND IT OPENS

Opens in about half a second with a soft spring. Click again and it folds back, a bit quicker.

3HOW IT GETS ONTO YOUR DESKTOP

In Aang's bubble, press and hold a job for half a second. A gold ring fills, the job comes away under your pointer, and the jobs left behind close up with a tiny bounce. No empty gap.

4HOW IT GOES BACK

Drag it onto Aang. As you get close, Aang and his bubble light up white and a space opens in the job list. Let go and it slides in. (If his bubble is closed, it opens as you get near.)

5WHAT IT SHOWS

Working, paused and finished, each folded. The finished one plays: Nice shrinks it away.

WHEN CLAUDE NEEDS YOU

The bar gets a small blue chip. Click it and Aang's bubble opens with the question, so you answer where you always do.

6SEE-THROUGH: HOW YOU'LL CONTROL IT

First, what it looks like over your game. Same job, open, at full and at 55%.

Four ways to set it, all with no extra buttons on the bar. Each one plays on a loop; the close-up shows the one thing that's different.

AScroll the wheelMY PICK

Mouse over the job, roll the wheel down to fade it, up to bring it back. A tiny number shows by the pin for a second.

Nothing added to the bar at all.

Fast, and you can fine-tune it.

You have to know it's there (Aang tells you once).

BA slider when you hover

Put your mouse on the job and a thin slider appears next to the pin. Drag it down to fade.

Easy to find: you see it the moment you hover.

Something pops out every time you touch the job.

CRight-click menu

Right-click the job, pick 100, 80, 60 or 40%.

Plain and hard to get wrong.

Two clicks, and only four set levels.

DDouble-click the pin

Each double-click steps it: 100, 75, 50, then back to 100.

Uses the one button it already has.

A double-click is also a click, so the pin flicks on and off too.

It can never disappear. It stops at 30%, so you can always find it and drag it home. (The video pop-out stops at 20%; this one is mostly words, so a little more.)

It remembers. Each job keeps its fade when you put it back and pull it out again.

AND WHEN YOUR MOUSE IS ON IT?

Should it go solid while your mouse is over it? My pick: no. With A you set the fade with your mouse on top, so it has to show you the real fade. It can be a switch in Settings later.
