1 · THE LIGHT BRINGS YOUR EMAIL

Click the Email box. The light appears beside it holding your email (a letter). Roll the scroll wheel on it, or press ↑ ↓, to flick between your emails. Click it or press TAB. No hints on the light itself: they live in Settings.

2 · ONE CLICK, WHOLE FORM

On a form, the light carries your name card and fills every box it knows at once, a soft shine running down. Change anything after.

3 · THE KEY NEEDS YOUR PIN

Bitwarden is locked. Click the Password box: the key's place in the address bar shows a padlock, it nods and calls you (two soft notes), and the bar says what it wants. Type your 4-digit PIN on the number pad, no clicking: one small light comes on beside the padlock for each digit, and the padlock warms a little each time. On the fourth, the shackle lifts, it blooms and settles into the glowing key, the warm unlock sound plays, and the light brings your password. (Any 4 digits work here; 0000 shows a wrong try.)

4 · A NEW LOGIN

You signed in with a new password. The key itself glows warm (no new icon). Point at it and "Save" appears; click it and Samara shows exactly what it will keep, so you can fix anything first. Ignore it and the glow fades.
