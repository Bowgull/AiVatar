3 · THE KEY NEEDS YOUR PIN

Bitwarden is locked. Click the Password box: the key in the address bar calls you (a small nod, a glow, two soft notes) and the bar says what it wants. Type your PIN on the number pad, no need to click anything: each of your 4 digits fills the key with more light. The fourth blooms it open, the warm unlock sound plays, and the light brings your password. (Any 4 digits work here; 0000 shows a wrong try.)

4 · A NEW LOGIN

You signed in with a new password. The key glows warm. Point at it and "Save" appears; click it and Samara shows exactly what it will keep, so you can fix anything first. Ignore it and the glow fades.
