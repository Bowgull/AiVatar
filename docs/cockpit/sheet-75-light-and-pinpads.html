SHEET 75 - THE LIGHT AND ITS PIN PAD

You liked the companion light, so here it's the whole of autofill: it carries your papers and keys, it shows Bitwarden's state by how it looks, and when Bitwarden is locked it brings a PIN pad of Samara's own. Each pad is laid out like your real number pad, so the key you press lights on screen; you can click it too. Each pad below is already open, with four of six digits in: click inside the shop and type two more on your number pad to see it open. "Start again" shows the light filling your email and key while Bitwarden is open.

HOW THE LIGHT LOOKS

AWAKE

Bitwarden is open. Warm gold, wings going.

CARRYING

It holds what fits this box: a letter, a key.

ASLEEP

Bitwarden has locked. Dim blue, dozing. A PIN wakes it.

NOTHING FITS

No light at all. It never shows up just to say no.

A · THE WOODEN KEYS

Samara's own wood and gold, the ⋯ menu's frame

The light opens a small number pad of carved wood keys beside the box. Press a key on your keyboard and the same key sinks and glows from inside. Gold diamonds fill as you go.

B · THE TRAVEL LOCK

a chest padlock with six rolling dials

A brass padlock: each digit rolls a dial into place with a click (it lands on a mark, never your digit, so nobody behind you reads your PIN). On the sixth, the shackle springs open and your key drops into the box.

C · THE SHRINE STONES

Breath of the Wild's shrines, Moria's moonlit lines

Nine carved stones in the number pad's places, each with its own rune and note. Press a key and its stone wakes in moonlight and sings; your PIN becomes a little tune your hand knows.
