What I changed from your idea, and why
  • The pause key is gone from the hover keys: the glass already does it, so the keys are just +1 / +5 / ✕.
  • Click vs hold on the same glass: a press under a quarter second is a click (pause). The ring only shows after 0.15 s, so a click never flashes it.
  • Restart is a hold, never a click, so one stray click can't wipe a running timer. At time's up a click is fine (nothing to lose).
  • Rename and Custom time need the keyboard. The timer never takes your keys today (so WoW keeps them). It would take them only while that small box is open, and give them back on Enter, Esc or a click away.

7THE SOUNDS: DO THEY CLASH?

Yes, v1's did. Its time's-up climbed E-G-C on the same wooden bars as Job complete, and ended on the same note: you'd hear "job done" when it was the pizza. And today a timer plays the old needs-you, 10 dB louder than everything else. The fix: your chimes use C, D, E, G and A, and none of them repeats a note. So the timer gets the one note nobody uses, F, struck three times evenly, like a clock. Same wood, same level.
