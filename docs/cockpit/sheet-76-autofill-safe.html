My honest pick is A. All three keep the same four rules, so you are only choosing where the offer appears. Each one plays its three moments on its own; click a moment to hold it.

PIN IN THE BARTyped in Samara's own address bar, the one place a website can never draw, so no site can fake it.

A · THE LIGHT BY THE BOX

MY PICK

The light you liked, kept small. It flies to the box you click with what fits; click it or press Tab. When Bitwarden is locked it flies up into the address bar and sleeps by the key: type your PIN, pips fill in the bar, it wakes and fills.

B · ALL IN THE BAR

Nothing on the page at all. When the box you clicked has an answer, the key in the address bar glows and says what it has; Tab fills. The PIN works the same as A. The quietest and the safest, but your eyes have to go up to the bar.

C · ALREADY IN THE BOX

Your answer shows faded right where you're looking, with a small Tab mark; Tab takes it, typing makes it go away. The PIN works the same as A. The fastest to read, but the least "Samara": it has no character of its own.
