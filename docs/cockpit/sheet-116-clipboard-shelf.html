SHEET 116 · THE SATCHEL

v2.2 · 1 Nov · the keys rise above the clip you hover, so they never cover it; no hotkey label in the Satchel

Everything you copy, snip or drop is kept in the Satchel. It is not on your screen until you want it: hover the middle of the right edge and a small tab peeks out, click it; or press Ctrl + Alt + V anywhere. Click a clip to copy it, drag it into any app, pin it to the top, keep it as a note, or ask Aang about it.

1THE SATCHEL, LIVE

Move the mouse to the middle of the right edge of the box below: the tab peeks out. Click it. Or click the Ctrl + Alt + V key (or press it): then just type to find a clip, use the arrows, and Enter copies it and closes. A click on a clip copies it and the Satchel stays open. It closes with Esc, a click outside, the hotkey again, or when your mouse wanders off. Hover a clip: it makes room above itself and its keys rise into it, the word of the key under your mouse beside them.

2THE TAB: THREE LOOKS

It only shows while your mouse is in that spot (and never while a game runs). Drop a file or a picture on it and the Satchel opens and keeps it.

3THE ICONS, AND THEIR WORDS

Hand-placed pixel icons in the notes' style. Hover one on a clip and its word shows, so Pin and Keep as a note are never mixed up. Ask keeps Aang's face.

4WHAT LIVES IN IT

WordsAnything you copy: a line from Discord, a command, an address. Long ones fold to three lines. LinksWith the site's own icon and the page title, like your bookmarks. PicturesCopied pictures and screenshots. Drag one straight into Discord, Photoshop or a folder. SnipsEvery Snipit snip, on pale blue paper (Aang's colour). It still lands as a chip in your typing box first; the Satchel just keeps it. FilesFiles you copy in Explorer. Dragging one out moves the real file, not a copy. PinnedPinned clips sit at the top and never clear. Everything else clears after 7 days. From the MacClips copied on the MacBook have lilac paper and say so.

5HOW IT MOVES, AND AANG'S BUBBLE TOO

Learned from Edge-Drop's code: the Satchel never moves or resizes. A rounded mask grows out of the strip on a spring, which costs the computer almost nothing, so it stays smooth while a game runs. New clips drop in with a small settle and the rest glide down; the tab peeks out with a little bounce; nothing jumps. The same spring can open Aang's bubble from the corner nearest Aang: try it below.

6WITH THE REST OF AANG

Snipit, highlight and drop work exactly as now (one chip, Aang asks one line). The Satchel is where they stay afterwards. Keep as a note opens your quick note (the same one as Ctrl + *) with the clip already in it, ready for a word or a category. Ask Aang "what was that link I copied yesterday?" and he answers with one line and a door that opens the Satchel at that clip.

7WHAT IT NEVER KEEPS

Password managers (Bitwarden, 1Password, KeePass)Bank and money sitesAnything an app marks private

Kept on this PC, locked with Windows' own encryption (pictures too, which Edge-Drop leaves open). Shared with the Mac over Tailscale only; private clips never travel. Aang never reads it on his own and never while a game runs. Pasting is yours: Aang puts a clip on your clipboard or you drag it; he never presses Ctrl+V for you (that could land in WoW).
