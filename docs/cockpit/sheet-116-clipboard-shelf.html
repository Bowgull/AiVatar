SHEET 116 · CLIPBOARD SHELF

v1 · 1 Nov · live: hover the gold strip, press the keys, try slow motion

Everything you copy, snip or drop lands on one shelf on the edge of your screen. Hover the edge and it slides out. Click a clip to copy it back, drag it into any app, stack clips together, pin the ones that matter, or keep one as a note. Aang can find things on it when you ask.

WHAT TO PICK (1) Its name: Shelf (my pick), Satchel, or Scroll case. (2) Which edge: right (my pick) or left. (3) Unpinned clips clear after 7 days (my pick), 1 day or 30 days. (4) Yes or no: Aang's bubble opens with the same spring (section 3).

1THE SHELF, LIVE

Rest the mouse on the gold strip on the right edge: it opens after a moment. It stays open while you are near it and closes about a second after you move away, so it never flickers. The strip breathes softly when something new landed. Hover a clip for its keys: Copy, Pin, Keep as a note, Ask Aang about it, Bin.

2WHAT LIVES ON IT

WordsAnything you copy: a line from Discord, a command, an address. Long ones fold to three lines. LinksWith the site's own icon and the page title, like your bookmarks. PicturesCopied pictures and screenshots. Drag one straight into Discord, Photoshop or a folder. SnipsEvery Snipit snip, on pale blue paper (Aang's colour). It still lands as a chip in your typing box first; the shelf just keeps it. FilesFiles you copy in Explorer. Dragging one out moves the real file, not a copy. StacksDrop a clip on another and they stack; the stack fans open when you hover it. From the MacClips copied on the MacBook have lilac paper and say so.

3HOW IT MOVES, AND AANG'S BUBBLE TOO

Learned from Edge-Drop's code: the shelf never moves or resizes. A rounded mask grows out of the strip on a spring, which costs the computer almost nothing, so it stays smooth while a game runs. New clips drop in with a small settle and the rest glide down; nothing jumps. The same spring can open Aang's bubble from the corner nearest Aang: try it below.

4WITH THE REST OF AANG

Snipit, highlight and drop work exactly as now (one chip, Aang asks one line). The shelf is where they stay afterwards. A clip dropped on a note becomes part of the note. Ask Aang "what was that link I copied yesterday?" and he answers with one line and a door that opens the shelf at that clip.

5WHAT IT NEVER KEEPS

Password managers (Bitwarden, 1Password, KeePass)Bank and money sitesAnything an app marks privateAnything while Pause is on

Kept on this PC, locked with Windows' own encryption (pictures too, which Edge-Drop leaves open). Shared with the Mac over Tailscale only; private clips never travel. Aang never reads it on his own and never while a game runs. Pasting is yours: Aang puts a clip on your clipboard or you drag it; he never presses Ctrl+V for you (that could land in WoW).
