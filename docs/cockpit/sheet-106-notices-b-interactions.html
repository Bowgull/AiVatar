THE RULES IT FOLLOWS

  • Drops from under whatever caused it, joined to it by a small tab: the download key, the shield, the menu.
  • Teal done, blue needs you, red went wrong. Only "needs you" plays a sound. Claude always shows its orange spark on a white socket.
  • Big ones stay until you click a button. Small ones slide back by themselves after 4 seconds, and wait while your mouse is on them.
  • Every download's picture is a handle: drag it into Finder, an email or a chat. Dropping it counts as done.
  • One at a time. More waiting shows a count ("1 of 3"); the next drops when you finish this one.
