SHEET 88 - THE ADDRESS BAR

Samara guesses what you want as you type, the way Chrome does, with your own tabs and history first. And three widths for the bar, side by side.

1 · TODAY: ONLY "SEARCH THE WEB"

2 · AS YOU TYPE: YOUR OWN THINGS FIRST, THEN 4 GUESSES

The guesses come from your search engine: Brave here, Yandex in the secret window. Your letters stay plain; the rest of each guess is bold.

3 · THE ARROW KEYS: THE BAR SHOWS THE LINE YOU'RE ON

Down to "pixel art", and the bar says it; Enter searches it. Esc brings back what you typed.

4 · A SITE YOU'VE BEEN TO, FINISHED AS YOU TYPE

Type "red" and the rest of reddit.com fills in, highlighted. Enter goes there; keep typing and it steps aside. Only your own history and bookmarks ever fill in, never a guess.

5 · THE BAR'S WIDTH

A · Today: 1108 wide, about three quarters of the window.

B · Centred, 760 wide, like Safari: the wood shows on both sides.

C · From the left, 860 wide: beside the buttons, the wood shows on the right.

THE RULES

  • Each letter goes to your search engine as you type, in both windows (your pick).
  • At most 4 guesses, always under your own tabs, bookmarks and history.
  • Only your own history and bookmarks finish an address; a guess never does.
  • In the secret window, its own history only after your PIN; Yandex's guesses before and after.
