SHEET 105 · MARKS WHEN AANG IS ON HIS SIDE

Docked on a side edge, Aang lies sideways but his mark stays standing up next to his head. Here are three ways to draw it.

His real frame and the real marks, drawn the way the Mac app draws them. Shown at the left edge; the right edge is the mirror.

AToday

The mark stands upright beside his face, so it looks stuck to the side of his head.

BTurned with himOPTION

The mark turns with him and floats over his head, the same as when he stands at the bottom. It reads as part of him.

CStanding, above himOPTION

The mark stays upright but moves above him against the edge, like a flag on the wall. Easy to read, never on his face.
