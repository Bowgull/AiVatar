SHEET 74 - AUTOFILL, OUR OWN WAY

No list popping out of the box, and no separate PIN window. Five ways Samara could do it, each one live: click into the boxes, use Tab and the arrow keys, type on the number pad. Sound is soft and only plays on a key you press. They can mix: for example 1 for typing, 4 for the PIN, 5 for long forms.

1 · ALREADY IN THE BOX

quietest of all

Click a box and your answer is already there, faded. TAB takes it. ↑ ↓ flick through others right inside the box, like Zelda's quick-select. Start typing and it gets out of the way.

2 · THE COMPANION LIGHT

Zelda's fairy, or Aang's own little light

A small light flies from the key in the address bar to the box you clicked and waits beside it. Point at it to see what it carries; touch it (or TAB) and it fills, with a spark. On the password box it brings your key.

3 · THE ACTION PROMPT

Breath of the Wild's "A · Open"

Beside the box you clicked, one small prompt, the way Hyrule tells you what a button does. TAB fills. Nothing covers the page.

4 · THE LOCK IN THE BOX

the PIN played like an ocarina

Bitwarden has locked. The password box itself becomes the lock: type your PIN right in it on the number pad. Each digit lights a gold pip and plays a soft note; the right six notes open it and your password drops in. (Here, any 6 digits open it; 000000 shows a wrong try.)

5 · ONE PRESS, WHOLE PAGE

for long forms

On a form Samara knows, the key in the address bar glows. One press (click it, or CTRL + ENTER) fills every box in turn, a shine running down the form like a spell landing.
