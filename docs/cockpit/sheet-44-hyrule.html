SHEET 44 · SAMARA, BY WAY OF HYRULE

Just ideas, just mockups: what Samara's language could become with Breath of the Wild and Tears of the Kingdom in it. Three directions for the whole browser first, then a dozen of Samara's own features re-imagined through the games' best moments: Recall, Ultrahand, the stamina wheel, shrines, the Depths' lightroots, the camera, the cooking pot. Everything is drawn fresh and only inspired by the games: no Nintendo artwork, symbols or words. Nothing here is built.

3 · THE REST OF THE LANGUAGE

Type and glyphs
Our own glyphs (rings, diamonds, crossed circles), used like the games use theirs: on frame caps, as loading marks, as section badges. Labels stay in your pixel font; long text stays readable, never in a made-up alphabet.
Sound
  • A soft rising chime when a tab group is "cooked"
  • A two-note glint when a page goes dark
  • The sensor's ping in find-in-page, quiet and optional
  • A tiny "found" sparkle for the hidden friends
All original sounds, short, and off unless you turn sounds on (Samara's Sounds switch).
Where it should never go
  • Never slower: every animation is under half a second unless it is a celebration you can ignore
  • Never in your game: none of it plays while WoW is in front
  • Never Nintendo's art, symbols, music or words: inspired, not copied
  • Your wood, pixels and parchment stay the base (direction C)
