WHAT I HAD WRONG

Sheet 9 kept the rectangular game panel and added comic beads to it, which is why it read as neither one thing nor the other. A comic balloon is not a rounded rectangle. It is an organic shape, with an even bold ink line all the way round, a near-white fill, centred lettering, and a tail that tapers to a point.

The lettering trade only specifies two numbers and both are used here: the air round the text is about one letter width, and the tail reaches half to sixty per cent of the way to the speaker, with a line continued past its tip landing on his mouth. There is no standard ink weight anywhere in that literature, so it is 5px against 15px type.

The tail is drawn in front of the balloon and overlapping it, so its fill covers the balloon's own ink line and the two edges carry on from it. That open join is what makes a tail look part of the balloon rather than glued to it.
