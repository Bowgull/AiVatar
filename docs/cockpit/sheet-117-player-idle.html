SHEET 117 · PLAYER IDLE MODE

v3 · 1 Nov · scenes repainted wide; three ways to fit a square album cover

Leave the mouse off the player for 20 seconds and it goes idle. Hover a player to wake it. Nothing is cut off any more: the scenes are painted wide to fit the window exactly, and square album covers always show whole.
