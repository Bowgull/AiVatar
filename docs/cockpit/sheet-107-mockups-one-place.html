Sheet 107 · Mockups, one place

One way in: Mockups, a single tab in your trail. Inside, a tab for each project with its logo, topics as sub headings, a Done section in every project, and the sheet itself opens right there at its true size. The old mockup list in the trail goes.

1 · THE TRAIL: ONE MOCKUPS TAB

Mockups sits in your trail like any tab. The gold number says how many sheets arrived that you haven't looked at. No list of mockup tabs under it any more, and no LIVE PAGES: every sheet, from any project, is inside Mockups.

2 · INSIDE MOCKUPS

Click a project tab. Its topics are the sub headings; built and parked sheets fold into Done at the bottom of that project. Pick a sheet and it opens on the right, at its true size, with its versions and its mark. Back returns to where you were.

5 · SHARP, NOT BLURRY

  • The sheet: shown at its true size (100%), never shrunk to fit. If it's wider than the space, it scrolls.
  • Pictures: taken at full size (today they're saved at 320 by 200 and stretched to 820 wide, which is the blur).
  • Logos and icons: never scaled up. Pixel art at whole-number sizes only; a photo-style logo only ever scaled down.
