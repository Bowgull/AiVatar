SHEET 73 - AUTOFILL, THREE WORLDS

Sheet 72 felt cheap. Here's what autofill actually is in the worlds you love, then three ways to draw it. Every direction is live: click a box on the shop, move with the arrow keys, press Enter or click to pick. Press "Lock Bitwarden" and pick a login, then type any 6 digits on the number pad. Sign in to see the new-key moment.

YOUR PAPERSYour name, email, phone, home. What a traveller carries and shows at the gate.
YOUR KEY RINGOne key per site, from your Bitwarden chest. You pick which key fits this door.
THE WORDWhen the chest has locked, it wants your word: the PIN. Quick, on the number pad.
A NEW KEYYou signed in somewhere new. Samara offers to keep the key, like finding an item.

A · THE SATCHEL

Stardew Valley's inventory, Breath of the Wild's item rows

Your papers and keys are items in a satchel, each in its own slot. The cursor slot lifts and bobs; a parchment tag says what it is. The PIN is a lock with six tumblers that drop as you type. A new login pops up bottom-left like an item you picked up.

B · THE DOOR

Lord of the Rings: the doors of Moria, lines that only show under moonlight

Dark stone, thin moonlit lines. The chosen row is underlined with a glowing thread. The PIN is an arched door with six runes; each digit wakes one, and on the last the whole door glows and opens. A new key is offered in the same stone, quietly, top-right.

C · ITEM GET

Zelda: Ocarina's pulsing cursor, rupees, a key held up high

A dark Zelda menu with the four gold corners that pulse around what you point at. The PIN lights a rupee per digit (green, blue, red, purple, silver, gold). A new login is the classic moment: the key raised in light, and a text box that says you got it.
