5 · PAID SERVICES, AND THE SHADOW CATCH

Prime, Crunchyroll and Netflix all play in the pop-out. Tested on 2026-10-04 and you watched it yourself. But your Windows machine is a Shadow cloud PC, so there is one real catch, and it is honest to show it rather than hide it.

The purple plaque means a paid service. DUB is there because Aang remembers you only watch dubbed, and Simkl cannot store that, so he keeps it himself.

Every skip says so. A small plaque appears for three seconds with an Undo, so nothing is ever skipped silently. If it skipped something you wanted, one click puts you back.

THE CATCH, IN PLAIN WORDS

Aang cannot see this one

Your PC is in a data centre. Everything on it reaches you as a video stream. Paid video refuses to travel in a stream like that, so Shadow showed an error instead.

The fix, and it works

Shadow's own instructions say to turn off a graphics shortcut for that window. We did, and the episode played perfectly, 1,500 frames in a minute with none dropped.

What it means day to day: nothing. It only affects this one window. The one odd part is that if Aang takes a picture of his own screen, paid video comes out black. So when we check whether a video works, you look, not him.

6 · SENDING IT TO THE MACBOOK

You play Shadow on your MacBook, so the Mac is always right there. Aang can open something on it directly: a backup if a paid service ever refuses, and useful on its own when you would rather watch on the Mac's own screen.

What I can do there

Open a link on a service you approved. That is the whole list. No files, no commands, nothing else, and only over your own private network between the two machines.

WHEN AANG OFFERS IT HIMSELF

Netflix will not play in the pop-out until you do the free sign-up. Want it on the MacBook instead?

He only offers this when something genuinely will not play here. He never quietly moves your video to another machine.
