SHEET 29 - SAMARA

Samara is the real name for a maple "helicopter" seed, the one that spins down on the wind. Six ways to draw it as the browser's mark. None is round, none uses orange on purple (Firefox's), and each is pixel art with an ink outline, light from the top-left. Under each: real size on a dark and a light taskbar, and in grey (the squint test). Underneath: how the name and mark sit together.
