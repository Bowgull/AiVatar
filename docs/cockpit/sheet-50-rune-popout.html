THE MOTION RULES (EVERYWHERE)

  • Growing: about 520 ms, a soft spring that overshoots a hair and settles.
  • Shrinking: quicker, about 340 ms, no bounce.
  • Small changes (a colour, a glow, a key lighting up): about 300 ms.
  • Several things appearing: each one 50 to 60 ms after the one before, never all at once.
  • The curve: Samara's own ease, cubic-bezier(.32,.72,0,1): quick off the mark, gentle landing.
  • Loops (the rune's glow and shimmer): slow, 2 to 4 seconds, and nothing moves if Windows asks for less motion.
