Sheet 99 · Send to TV, in the TV's own apps

Send to TV opens the show in the TV's own app, signed in as you. The small TV in the address bar fills up as each step finishes, so you always know it's working. Every picture is Samara itself, on today's bar (the bar redesign is still open).

1 · SEND TO TV, IN THE MENU

On Netflix, the first choice is the TV's own Netflix app. "Just the video" isn't offered here, because Netflix's video is locked to the page. The whole window stays as the fallback.

2 · THE LOADING SIGN: THE TV ICON FILLS UP

No words, nothing extra in the bar. The TV's screen fills a third at a time, with a bright edge where it has reached. When the show is playing, it lights up fully with its soft glow, like today.

3 · LINKING, ONLY THE FIRST TIME

The first time you send, the TV shows a code and Samara asks for it, hanging from the TV icon. You type it once and never again.

4 · IF THE TV DOESN'T WAKE

Never silent. It says what happened and offers the whole window, which works with the TV asleep. It uses the notices' red for "went wrong".
