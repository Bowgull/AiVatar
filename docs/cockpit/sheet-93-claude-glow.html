Sheet 93 · Claude's glow

A calm sign that Claude is working in Samara. It shows only while Claude is clicking, typing or reading, then fades out, so when you see it, it means something. All four use Claude's own orange. Tap Acting and Quiet to compare.

1 · EMBER IN THE BAND

A thin ember line along the bottom of the toolbar, breathing slowly. A spark runs along it while Claude types.

Seen from anywhere on screen. Doesn't touch the page.
