SHEET 45 · AANG AND CLAUDE, BOTH WAYS

Everything from your interview, drawn on your real bubble and pet. Claude to Aang: needs you, a question, progress, done. Aang to Claude: your answer, your approval, a new job, picking an old one back up. Aang translates both ways: you see a plain decision; Claude gets a precise instruction; Claude's exact words are always one click away. The Claude mark is the door into that exact session, everywhere.

1 · THE WHOLE THING (PRESS PLAY)

Waymark needs a permission, you peek at Claude's words, look at the list of everyone waiting, allow it, answer the next one by typing, watch it work, and it finishes with a quest-complete.

2 · CLAUDE NEEDS YOU

A PERMISSION, TRANSLATED

The pet's own white ! pops over his head (only on the computer you are at). The bubble says it in plain words, with your four keys: Allow once, Always allow, No, No and... The Claude mark at the top opens Waymark itself in Claude; hover it and it says so. 2 MORE WAITING hangs under the bubble like a wooden tab.

CLAUDE'S OWN WORDS, ONE CLICK AWAY

For when you want to see exactly what it will run. Quiet by default, a small carved slot when open.

3 · SEVERAL AT ONCE: THE LIST

"2 MORE WAITING", OPENED

The Lantern, in the order of how stuck they are: permissions (Claude is frozen), then questions, then finished. Each row has the pet's own mark, the session, PC or MAC, and how long it has waited. Pick one and the bubble switches to it, keys and all.

A QUESTION, AND "TYPE SOMETHING ELSE"

Claude's multiple-choice questions become keys. No, and... or Type something else opens Aang's own typing box with a terracotta TO CLAUDE · Waymark chip, so you always know where your words go. Aang turns them into a proper reply.

This list is a pattern for all of Aang, not just Claude. The top thing is answered in the bubble; the rest wait in a priority list under it; picking one switches the bubble to it. Same order everywhere: stuck > questions > finished > news.
  • Emails waiting on a reply, job hunt cards to review, Discord mentions
  • Plan cards from two Claude jobs at once, downloads that need a decision, Samara asking for the camera or popups
  • Updates and the Waymark refresh, the morning brief's items

4 · STARTING A JOB THROUGH AANG

"HAVE CLAUDE FIX THE WAYMARK LOGIN BUG"

Aang names the job as a Claude link and asks how much to trust it, once per job: Ask me everything / Trust edits / Full trust (full trust is burnt orange, the colour for "careful"). Which computer is asked once per project, then just said, with Change.

BIG JOB: CLAUDE PLANS FIRST

Aang decides by size; you can always say plan it. The plan comes to the bubble as sheet 37's card: Go / Change / Not now. Small jobs just start.

5 · PROGRESS: WHERE IT LIVES (YOU ASKED FOR ADVICE)

MY PICKA small bar over Aang's head while a Claude job works, like a health bar over a character. You can read it out of the corner of your eye, it never talks, and a window over WoW is already free (6.1). Hover it for the name; click it and the bubble opens the full bars, the build-xp way (OVERALL and THIS STEP, step 3 of 6, time left). The bubble itself never shows progress unless you ask. Samara's band gets the same bar as a chip on the right while you are in the browser; hover it and the bars drop down.

ON THE PET, AND ONE LINE WHEN YOU CLICK IT

The bar comes from Claude's own to-do list: every finished task fills it. Gold, his working colour, in pixel segments; the newest segment softly blinks.

OPENED: THE BARS, WITH PAUSE TUCKED BEHIND ···

The Waymark mod's two bars, in wood. Pause, Open in Claude and the trust level live behind the ···, as you asked: there when you look, never in the way.

IN SAMARA'S BAND

A chip at the right end of the band: Claude's mark, the job, the bar. Hover drops the bars down; click opens the session.

6 · DONE: QUEST COMPLETE

ONE LINE, A RIBBON, THE NEXT STEP

A gold QUEST COMPLETE ribbon drops onto the bubble for two seconds with a few pixel sparkles, the plan's bar fills in the update glow's turquoise, Claude's terracotta tick sits over the pet. The obvious next thing is the gold key; What changed lists the files in plain words.

CLAUDE IS ALWAYS A LINK

Whenever Aang says Claude or names a Claude job, it carries Claude's mark and opens that session, like a site name opens Samara. Weeks later in the scroll-back too.

7 · AFTER A SHUTDOWN

"PICK BOTH UP?"

Automatic: when the PC goes down, Aang notes what was running and how far; when it is back, one question. Pick both up types "continue" into each exact session. Manual Pause stays behind the ··· on a job.

8 · THE PET'S MARKS, SMOOTHER

Your red and white system stays exactly as it is. Only the motion and colour get the Samara and Waymark polish: a squash-and-stretch pop, a soft halo in the mark's own colour that breathes, and a small count when more than one thing is waiting.

9 · THE SOUNDS, BY EAR

Today's "done" hits almost full volume with a sharp start, and the pet plays it through a Windows player with no volume control. Four new families to listen to, all much quieter with soft starts: wooden chimes, magic shimmer, music box, and your current two, softened. Each has Needs you, Done and Quest complete. A volume slider and mute go in Settings.
