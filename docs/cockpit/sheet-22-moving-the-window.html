Sheet 22: making the browser easy to move

Your real browser, as it is now. Nothing about the look changes unless the option says so.

= where you can grab and drag the window

Today

Only the thin wood strips above and below the buttons, and the bit of wood between the buttons, can be grabbed. About 10 pixels tall. That is why it is fiddly.

tiny grab strips

Option A: the wood around the page becomes a handleno look change

The wood frame around the page (top, sides, bottom) is already there and big. Make all of it grabbable. The page itself and the tab tray handle still click like normal. This is the biggest target for zero visual change.

grab anywhere on the wood

Option B: a narrower address barsmall look change

The address bar shrinks to about 60% and sits in the middle, like Safari. The wood on both sides of it becomes grab space. Web addresses still fit; long ones fade at the end like now.

grab here
or here

Option C: a carved grip plaquesmall look change

A small carved wood piece with grip notches, left of the back button, like the handle on a drawer. Obvious where to grab. Small target though.

grip

Option D: a taller wood lip on toplook change

The top wood band grows by about 14 pixels, a raised ridge above the buttons. A full-width grab strip, but the browser gets a bit taller and the page a bit shorter.

full-width ridge
My suggestion: A first. It keeps the look you like exactly as it is and gives you a big handle on every side. Add B too if you still want more room up top. C and D change the look, so only if A and B are not enough.
Also, either way: double-clicking the wood will maximize or restore, and dragging to a screen edge snaps it, like normal Windows windows.
