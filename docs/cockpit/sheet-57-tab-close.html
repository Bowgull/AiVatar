SHEET 57 · QUIETER TAB CLOSE

The dark circles go. Four quieter ways to close a tab, each beside today's drawer. In every picture your pointer is on the Bowgull/CereBro row, and Wowhead is the tab you're on.

WHAT TO PICK A (my pick), B, C or D.

True for all four
  • Ctrl+W still closes the tab you're on, and a middle-click on any row still closes it (it already does today).
  • The x is drawn small, but the spot you click stays 26 px square (30 in B), a touch bigger than today's circle. Easy to hit, even on a touch screen.
  • In A, C and D, tabbing to the x with the keyboard shows it with a gold ring, so it's never lost.
ASmall pixel x, no circleMY PICK

The circle is gone. A small hand-pixelled x in quiet wood ink, only on the row you point at and on your tab. It turns gold when your pointer is on it.

KEYBOARDCtrl+W closes your tab. Tab key reaches the x.TOUCHThe x is always on your tab, so a tap closes it. Other tabs: tap to open, then tap its x.
