The Update Key

Its only job: tell you, at a glance, "a browser update is ready, restart when you like." So it has to read in half a second out of the corner of your eye, and it can't look like any key already on the band. Every real browser landed on the same answer: an arrow, and a word.

Why an up arrow

Every key on your band is a 7 by 7 pixel glyph on the same plum key. The update glyph has to be in that family, and it can't look like one that's already there.

How the other browsers do it

ChromeThe settings button grows a word: "Update" or "Relaunch to update". It gets more urgent in three steps the longer you wait. Checked in Chromium's own code.
FirefoxA small green arrow badge on the menu button, and a "Restart to update" line at the top of the menu.
DiscordA green arrow pops into the top bar only when an update is ready. Click it and it restarts. The closest to what you asked for.
Vivaldi and ArcWords, not icons. Vivaldi puts "Restart Required" in the address bar. Arc puts an "Update" banner in the sidebar, only once it has downloaded.
What they all agree onAn arrow and a plain word. No mascots, no metaphors. It only shows when the update is ready, and it gets more urgent with time, never with animation.
What I changedThe game icons are gone; they made you work out what they meant. The bob and the breathing are gone; nothing moves on your screen while you read or play. Arrival uses the downloads key's exact glow, so the two keys feel like one family.
