What's going on here

Only when neededThe key is not there at all until a newer engine is out. Then it pops in with the downloads key's own spring and glows once, pushing the settings key over.
You pick the momentNothing restarts on its own. Restart now, or Later and it stays. Like Chrome, it tells you how long it has waited: gold, then amber after 4 days, then ember after a week.
The glow, redoneIt arrives with one glint across the key, like getting an item, then the icon just bobs a pixel now and then, the way a quest marker hovers. No flashing. A soft warm breath only after a week.
Three colour stylesA: the plum key stays and the icon itself ages. B: the plum key stays, a small cut gem in its corner ages. C: a wood key like the band, and a gold ring around it fills over the week.
Ready before it asksBy the time the key shows, the engine is already downloaded, checked against Electron's fingerprints and tested on your PC, so Restart takes seconds.
A big updateSame key, but the panel says it's a big one and lists what might change. Still your click.
Safety netIf the new engine fails to start, the pet goes back to the old one by himself and never offers that version again.
