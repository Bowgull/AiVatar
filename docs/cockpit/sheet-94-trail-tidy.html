Sheet 94 · A tidier Mockups trail

Everything the trail does today stays: the marks, Approve, the archive, the broom. Only the layout changes. Two ways to show the list (A and B), and one way to compare two versions side by side (C). The sheets shown are your real ones.

A · QUEST LOG

Two tabs, like a game's quest log: Waiting on you, and Done.

One line per sheet, and the versions become little squares instead of rows. Their colour is the stage. Hover one for its date, click to open it. Built and parked sheets move to Done by themselves.

B · BOOKSHELF

Each topic is a shelf, each sheet a book in its stage's colour. The notches on a spine are its versions.

You see the state of everything at a glance, with no text to read. Tap a book to pull it out: its name and its version squares.

C · SIDE BY SIDE

Pick two versions (click one square, then Shift-click another) and they open next to each other in the page.

Drag the gold handle to slide between them. Works with A or B. Shown here: the cat sheet, v1 (three cats) against v2 (the one cat).
