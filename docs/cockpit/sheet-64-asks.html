An ask lands with the question and its tag only (and, on an OK, the exact thing you would allow, so you can read it first). Rest the mouse on the bubble for 1.5 seconds and the keys slide up into it on a soft spring. Moving off before then does nothing. Once they are out they stay.

The wait1.5 s of the mouse resting anywhere on the bubble. Leave earlier and the timer starts over next time. No bar or countdown is drawn: the bubble just answers when you linger. The bubbleGrows to make room first, on the same spring the bubble already uses when it resizes (about 180 ms, no bounce past the edge). The keysEach row or key rises 8 px and fades in, 40 ms after the one before it, settling in about 260 ms with a small soft overshoot. Picture 3 is 90 ms in. AfterThe keys stay once they are out, even when the mouse leaves, so nothing flickers. A new ask starts at rest again. No mouseThe keyboard letters (A, Z...) still answer straight away, before the keys show.
