SHEET 31 - SAMARA, AS A REAL ICON

Drawn big and smooth, the way real app icons are made, then shown where an icon actually lives: your Mac dock (on a dark and a light wallpaper), the Windows taskbar at normal and 150% size, and a browser tab at 16 px. The grey tiles next to it are stand-ins for your other apps, so you can see how it holds up beside them. Each one is built from D (the carved stamp) and E (the lantern), the two you didn't hate.
